Owner & shareholder portal

A private-banking register, not an ERP. Same tokens, different settings: one column of generous cards, 16px body instead of 14, no dense tables in the primary view, and money always in full with the currency spelled out.

Reasoning · how this differs from the staff UI
  • Density halves. Rows go from 44px to 64px, body from 14px to 16px, and the page holds a max-width of 1080 even on a 1440 monitor. An owner checks this twice a month; nothing is worth compressing.
  • The share percentage leads. A proportion bar plus the number, because “12.5%” on its own does not tell a non-technical investor how much of the building is theirs.
  • Every deduction is named. The single thing that destroys owner trust is a net figure they cannot reconcile. The statement shows the arithmetic in order, one line per deduction, no aggregation into “other”.
  • What I deprioritised: live occupancy and operational status. Owners do not need today’s room board, and showing it invites them to manage the hotel.
