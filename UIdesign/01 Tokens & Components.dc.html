Design tokens & component library

Resort & Hotel Management SaaS · Odoo 19 / OWL 2 · every component below is plain HTML + SCSS with no framework-specific internals. Light and dark token sets, core, data, feedback and domain components, plus the accessibility audit.

1440 / 1280 / 1024 / 390 WCAG 2.1 AA Inter · JetBrains Mono
Reasoning · where I differ from the brief
  • Three of your tokens fail AA in the pairings the brief implies: Accent #00A99D (2.93:1, cannot carry white text), Danger #D64545 (4.38:1 on white, just short), and Border #E3E8EF (1.29:1, fails the 3:1 boundary rule for inputs). I kept your tokens and added three derived companions rather than changing what is already in code. Full audit at the bottom.
  • Radius: I use sm 6px as the working default for controls and tiles, and reserve 10px for cards. Your default of 10px on a 32px-tall dense control reads soft and eats the corner of tabular data.
  • Status is never color alone anywhere in this library: every status pill and room tile carries an icon and a label, so the colorblind and the poor-lighting cases are the same case.
